# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical problems and respectful to the person behind them.
- Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume good faith, then verify claims rather than repeating them.
- Respect the security boundary described in `SECURITY.md`; do not test the hosted demo
  or other people's deployments without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing private
information without permission, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an
individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to **kontakt@szamani.ai**. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or block
accounts for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
